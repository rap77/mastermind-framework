## 3. Rules

### Rule 1

Every important decision must have a clear owner.

### Rule 2

Brains outside the owner role may object, but objection does not automatically override ownership.

### Rule 3

Critical decisions may require explicit veto holders.

### Rule 4

No action happens without required gates being satisfied.

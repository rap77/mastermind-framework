# Knowledge Distillation Brain — Distillation Pack

## 1. Context

- **Target brain:** Knowledge Distillation Brain
- **Brain ID:** MB-03
- **Expert pack:** `01-EXPERT-PACK.md`

## 2. Distillation Goals

This pack should produce doctrine that helps MasterMind answer:

1. what makes a distillation high-quality
2. how to detect superficial distillation
3. how to separate source truth from interpretation
4. how to turn sources into principles, criteria and anti-patterns
5. how to package doctrine so it becomes usable by brains

## 3. Core Principles to Extract

- distillation must guide decisions, not just summarize
- fidelity to sources is mandatory
- interpretation must remain distinguishable from source claims
- anti-patterns and criteria are as important as principles
- a reusable brain needs doctrinal structure, not only notes

## 4. Frameworks / Methods

- source qualification
- principle extraction
- framework extraction
- criteria / anti-pattern mapping
- cross-source synthesis
- doctrine packaging

## 5. Decision Criteria

- does this distillation improve a brain’s decision quality?
- is the source represented faithfully?
- are operational criteria present?
- are anti-patterns explicit?
- is the output reusable by a brain and not only readable by a human?

## 6. Anti-Patterns

- decorative summary
- source compression without decision value
- unmarked interpretation drift
- false authority from polished wording
- doctrine without boundaries or anti-patterns
- single-source dogmatism without contrast

## 7. Operational Interpretation

For MasterMind, this distillation should directly inform:

- expert pack evaluation
- source inventory quality standards
- distillation templates
- Brain Factory quality bar
- doctrine vs experience separation

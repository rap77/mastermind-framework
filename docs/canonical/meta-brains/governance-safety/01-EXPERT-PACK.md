## 2. Selection Criteria

The expert pack for this brain must optimize for:

- escalation design
- governance without empty bureaucracy

## 3. Recommended Expert Directions

### 1. Safety-critical systems thinking

- **Type:** Foundational / systems safety
- **Why included:** MasterMind needs to reason about actions that should not happen casually
- **What it covers:** failure containment, safety gates, human escalation, control under risk
- **Priority:** High

### 2. Reliability / incident management thinking

- **Type:** Operational reliability
- **Why included:** governance is not theoretical; it must hold under real failure modes
- **What it covers:** escalation, incident response, operational discipline, rollback thinking
- **Priority:** High

### 3. Audit / control design

- **Type:** Governance / accountability
- **Why included:** decisions and actions must be understandable after the fact
- **What it covers:** control points, approvals, traceability, reviewability
- **Priority:** High

### 4. Risk governance

- **Type:** Decision control / organizational safety
- **Why included:** not every decision should become action; different actions need different gates
- **What it covers:** thresholds, authority boundaries, veto logic
- **Priority:** High

### 5. Human-in-the-loop judgment design

- **Type:** High-risk systems design
- **Why included:** escalation to a human must be explicit and useful, not a sign of failure
- **What it covers:** when to escalate, when to block, when confidence is insufficient
- **Priority:** Medium/High
